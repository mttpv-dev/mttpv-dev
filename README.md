### 🤖 About Me
I am a Robotics Software Engineer currently working at the intersection of robotics navigation, manipulation and vision. Deeply interested in how to automate processes and discover patterns in data through machine learning. My graduate research was focused on the development of an upper-limb exoskeleton for rehabilitation. Lately I have been exploring more about 3D computer vision, vision transformers and VLA models.
